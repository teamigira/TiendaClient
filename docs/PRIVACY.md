# Tienda POS privacy notice

DRAFT, have it reviewed against Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, 2022

**Who we are.** [Business name], [address], [phone], [email] ("we") make and
sell Tienda POS.

**Data stays on your computer.** Tienda stores your products, sales, staff
accounts and customer records (names, phone numbers, loyalty points) in a
database on the shop's own computer (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Tienda`). We do not
receive, see or store this data.

**What Tienda sends to us.**
- *Licensing:* nothing is sent automatically. You send us your computer's code
  (a fingerprint made from the computer name and network card; it contains no
  personal data) so we can issue a licence. We keep the code, the business name
  and the licence dates to provide renewals and support.
- *Update check (only if enabled):* the app asks our server whether a newer
  version exists. The request contains the app version and nothing else.
- *Support:* if you send us log files or a backup for support, we use them only
  to fix your problem and delete them afterwards.

**Your responsibilities as a shop.** You are the data controller for your
customers' information. Collect only what you need (for loyalty, a name and
phone number), tell customers why, keep the computer protected with a Windows
password, and delete customers who ask you to.

**Security.** Staff passwords are stored as one-way hashes. Backups are plain
database copies: keep the USB drive or cloud folder you use for them private.

**Contact.** Questions about privacy: [email].
